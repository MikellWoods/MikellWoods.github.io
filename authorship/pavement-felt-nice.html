Pavement.

It has held many forgotten strokes of an accident-ridden palate.

Time tells of me being another.

Before then the grass was warm and slightly sharp, as I dragged my body across the ground aimlessly. My fingers shoveling, pulling dirt and shrubs out of the Earth just to continue movement. Just black and white is all that was dared to witness.

These legs were beyond weak, dead weight, while my eyes were devilishly blind, with a cloudy sight.

It was cruel and tedious, having pulled with hope that salvation would come. For a long moment, it was just grass. Not a flower or a tree that brushed against my hand, just grass. It felt endless.

Before what could be believed was eternity, a rigid structure knocked into my hand. It was a road, made of asphalt. Salvation and human settlement.

I hauled myself up on the road and with all my might, tried to push my body up to stand up on these legs, but they did not possess the same will as my needs.

This dragging continued more desperately as to reach the other side of the road. I believed to have reached the end, but more so to assume I reached the end of the world.

The moment these hands crossed, it was nothing. The sharpest inclined as if to crawl on top of a building to its ledge. I fear I would almost throw myself off.

I pushed back slightly, but was still rummaging these hands on the downslope. Desperate to wrap these arms around the road, as I dare to wrap my head around what is upon me. There was no such wrapping that could suffice.

My dragging continued across the stream of the road around its ledge to make any sense of it. The road wasn't bearable anymore. Color dared to seep in brightly, blinding and overbearing.

A need to come back to the familiarity of the warm grass. It was the end. The grass was gone and the endless drop had emerged.

Both sides were now pits and I was stuck on a hot road. In the long panic, it took to much time to feel the heat emanating from the pavement. Now my predicament has been learned, the sizzling skin must be felt.

If dared to see, the unimaginable andoversaturated vibrant red that must be blistering on this body. The feeling of chemicals being poured over skin and letting it simmer for a fire. I kept dragging these legs and body with what movement was left.

These arms were becoming weaker, losing the same resolve they had before. Becoming as dead as the legs are. To just shut down. To dropped this face onto the scorching road.

Even as it rummages, melting this face, there was no bearing to move this body anymore. To wait and simmered, The pain of heat.

To not know any better, one must believe hell swallowed them whole once the grass left their feet. The feeling of blacking out, the blown out color of the environment around me, a rumbling and tingling running across this skin and bones.

It went throughout this body and then into the Earth. Pavement was rumbling. It was speeding and blurring. I was hit by a force I could never predict.

The force and speed threw all aside and mangled me. The machine did not stop or break, only forward. Like a ragdoll I slid across it, reaching the side of the road as this weight slowly dragged off the edge. This hand pathetically tried to grip the side, but did not hold it for even a moment.

I fell. I fell and the wind traveled around me. To linger broken from body to spirit. It could have been an eternal fall for all to be known. It was a drop into all I never knew.

I did not see again that day.

I did not feel again that day.

I did not live again that day.

Roadkill was that day.

To long for the feeling of the burn, the feeling of anything. To be left without it.

The pavement felt nice.

To the Unforeseen Acts of Nature,

~ M.W.
