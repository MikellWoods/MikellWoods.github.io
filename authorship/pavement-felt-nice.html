Pavement.

It has held many forgotten strokes of an accident-ridden palate.

Time tells of me being another.

Before then the grass was warm and slightly sharp, as I dragged my body across the ground aimlessly. My fingers shoveling, pulling dirt and shrubs out of the Earth as I continued moving. Just black and white is all I dared to see.

My legs were beyond weak, dead weight, while my eyes were devilishly blind, with a cloudy sight.

It was cruel and tedious, I pulled with hope that salvation would come. For a long moment, it was just grass. Not a flower or a tree that brushed against my hand, just grass. It felt endless.

Before what I believed was eternity, a rigid structure knocked into my hand. It was a road, the asphalt. Salvation and human settlement.

I dragged myself up on the road and with all my might, tried to push my body up to stand up on my legs, but my legs did not possess the same strength and will as my need to survive.

I continued dragging more desperately to reach the other side of the road. I believed to have reached the end, but more of I felt I reached the end of the Earth.

The moment my hand crossed, it was nothing. The sharpest inclined as if I crawled on top of a building to its ledge. I feared I would almost throw myself off.

I dragged back slightly, but was still rummaging my hands on the downslope. Desperate to wrap my arms around the road as I wrap my head around what is around me. There was no such wrapping that could suffice.

I continued dragging myself across the stream of the road around its ledge to make more sense of it. I couldn't bear the road anymore. Color dared to seep in brightly, blinding and overbearing.

I need to come back to the familiarity of the warm grass. It was the end. The grass was gone and the endless drop had emerged.

Both sides were now pits and I was stuck on a hot road. In the long panic, it took longer to realize the heat emanating from the pavement. Now that I've learned my predicament, I can feel my skin sizzling.

If I could see, I could only imagine the oversaturated vibrant red that must be blistering on my body. I felt as if chemicals were being poured over my skin and letting it simmer for a fire. I kept dragging my legs as well as my body with what movement I had.

My arms were becoming weaker, losing the same resolve they had before. Almost becoming as dead weight as my legs were. I just shut down. I dropped my face onto the brutal scorching road.

Even as it was burning my face, possibly melting, I couldn't bear to move my body anymore. I waited and simmered, I felt nothing, but heat.

If I didn't know any better, I'd believe hell swallowed me whole once I walked off the grass. I felt as if I was blacking out, I could feel the blown out color of the environment around me, a rumbling and tingling running across my skin and throughout my body.

It went throughout my body and then into the Earth. Pavement was rumbling. It was speeding and blurring. I was hit by a force I could never predict.

The force and speed threw me aside and mangled me. The machine did not stop or break, only forward. Like a ragdoll I slid across it, reaching the side of the road as my weight slowly dragged off the edge. My hand pathetically tried to grip the side, but did not hold it for even a moment.

I fell. I fell and the wind traveled across my body. I linger broken from body to spirit. It could have been an eternal fall for all I knew. It was a drop into all I never knew.

I did not see again that day.

I did not feel again that day.

I did not live again that day.

Roadkill was that day.

To long for the feeling of the burn, the feeling of anything. I am left with none.
