Get To Know Me: ▾

Hello, I am Mike'll Woods, and this website is my portfolio/nexus for my creative works. I am an amateur filmmaker, writer, photographer, videographer, graphic designer, video editor, and front-end web developer.

I have been freely editing videos & photos before I was a High School Freshman. Leading to having over 5-6 years of amateur editing experience in total as of 2026. Throughout my schooling, I took multiple Digital Media classes. For my Photography Class, I got my certification for Adobe Photoshop in 2023. In my Video Editing Class, I got my certification in Adobe Premiere Pro in 2024, and this led me to ultimately obtain my certification in Adobe Video Design in 2024. In 2025, I was making graphics & editing videos for my High School's News Class, which would be shown to students every morning, each week.

I took my High School's coding class, which led to my certification in The Fundamentals of HTML, CSS, & JavaScript and Advanced JavaScript by the Operation Spark Program in 2023-2024. I've been making small projects for fun & grades, but now. This website is fully managed & coded by me, one of my biggest projects in a sense. In 2025, I graduated from high school at 17 years old with A-B Honor Roll and a 3.7+ GPA.

I attended college for filmmaking from 2026 to 2028. Mainly being picked for the roles of the video editor & director of photography, while of course lending a helping hand in the pre-production, active production, and post-production processes. Most of my tasks were creating digital graphics such as logos/labels, assisting or being the main video editing lead using DaVinci Resolve, and operating the camera while helping with general set assignments

In my creative free time, I write short works of fiction, practice my video and photo editing, test photography and videography techniques, make content for multiple YouTube channels, all with different styles, experiment with music to invoke certain feelings, and lastly network with online creators like editing, VFX, writing, and general filmmaking YouTubers.

Thank you for visiting my site. I hope you enjoy my work & website style. (CRT Glassmorphic, a custom theme of classic retro & modern technology I thought of. Pretty cool, right?)
